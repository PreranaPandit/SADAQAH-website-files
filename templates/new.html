THE FIRST LITTLE STEPS

Begin gently.

There's no perfect pace. Here are three simple places to start.

01

Learn one prayer at a time

Begin with the basics of salah and practise with patience. Small, steady steps count.

02

Read a little Qur’an daily

Explore accessible translations and reflect on one verse or passage that speaks to you.

03

Find people who listen

It’s okay to ask questions. Seek supportive, trustworthy guidance and kind company.
