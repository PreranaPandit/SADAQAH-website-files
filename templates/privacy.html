Privacy at SADAQAH.

What we collect

When you send us an inquiry, we collect your name, email and the details you choose to share, including optional phone, date, guest count, budget or basket items.

Why we collect it

We use these details to respond to your question, support request or event/product inquiry. An inquiry does not place a paid order or book catering.

How it is stored

Submissions are stored in the store's configured database. Authorized store administrators can view and update their status. We do not provide a public inquiries list. Protect your site with a strong admin password, secret key, HTTPS, and proper database backups.

Retention and removal

The site owner is responsible for setting a retention schedule, responding to privacy inquiries and deleting records when appropriate. For a removal request, use our contact page.

Site cookies

A necessary session cookie is used for basic form protection and administrator authentication. A local browser basket saves your selected items on your own device.

This policy is a launch-ready draft and should be reviewed against the shop's actual jurisdiction and practices before public launch.
